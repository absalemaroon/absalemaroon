## Absalex Labs Projects

Through Absalex Labs, I develop and maintain open-source tools and resources for the security community. This section will be updated as projects are released and made publicly available.

### Current Focus Areas

My software development efforts concentrate on:
- **Security Analysis Tools**: Tools for analyzing and auditing blockchain and distributed systems
- **Educational Resources**: Frameworks and guides for understanding security concepts
- **Security Automation**: Scripts and utilities for automated vulnerability detection and remediation
- **Community Tools**: Utilities that benefit the broader cybersecurity and blockchain communities

### Contributing

I welcome collaboration with researchers and developers who share an interest in advancing security. If you're interested in contributing to any Absalex Labs projects, please reach out through the contact information provided on this site.

More information about specific projects will be added here as they become available. Follow my blog for updates on new releases and developments.
