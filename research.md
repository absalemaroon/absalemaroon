### Research Focus Areas

My research interests include:
- **Blockchain Security**: Identifying and mitigating vulnerabilities in blockchain protocols and smart contracts
- **Distributed Systems**: Security aspects of distributed computing and consensus mechanisms
- **Cryptography**: Practical applications of cryptographic techniques in securing digital assets
- **Cyber Threat Intelligence**: Monitoring and analyzing emerging threats in decentralized systems

### Speaking Engagements & Events

I actively participate in conferences, workshops, and community events focused on cybersecurity and blockchain technology. My contributions include:
- Security research presentations
- Technical workshops on blockchain security best practices
- Participation in cybersecurity forums and discussion panels
- Collaboration with academic institutions on security initiatives

For updates on upcoming presentations and research activities, please check back here or follow my blog.
